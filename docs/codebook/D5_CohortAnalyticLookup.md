Notes:

1. Analyses in the file: estimand 1 (composite covid_diagnosis_or_death) in main, supplement, sensitivity1 (IPCW) and sensitivity2 (best/worst case scenarios); estimand 2 (covid_diagnosis, compliant_exposed == TRUE) in main and supplement.
2. Codes: -99 missing or no eligible cases; -88 fallback or model non-convergence; -77 non-reportable. In this file, -88 appears in 2 rows, both for the IPCW sensitivity analysis (tables 6.e and 7.e), in the ir, rr and VE columns. -77 does not appear. Codes are copied into VE columns where the RR is invalid.
3. Rate and person-year values are reported as produced by CohortAnalytics; units to confirm.

## Processing steps

1. create_cohort_analytic_outcomes.R builds the analysis combinations from the design metadata (source_D4, estimand, source_weight, condition, weight_col) and assigns combo_key.
2. CohortAnalytics computes incidence, risk ratio, risk difference and time columns for each combination (log-binomial model for RR).
3. VE is derived from the RR columns as described in the codebook.
4. The table is written to data/D5_results/DEAP_D5_CohortAnalyticLookup.csv.
